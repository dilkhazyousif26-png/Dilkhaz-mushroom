Growing Process

Prepare substrate

Substrate Preparation Straw or sawdust is pasteurized to eliminate contaminants. The substrate is soaked, drained, and prepared for inoculation in a clean environment.


Add spawn

Spawning & Inoculation Mushroom spawn (grain colonized with mycelium) is mixed thoroughly into the substrate and packed into growing bags with air filters.


Incubation

Bags are stored in a dark, warm room (20–25°C) for 14–21 days. White mycelium colonizes the substrate completely during this phase.with high humidity (85-95%), fresh air exchange, and indirect light. Mushroom pins appear within days.


Harvest

Mushrooms are hand-harvested at peak freshness, weighed,


Package

packed, and delivered to local markets and restaurants within hours.

Health & Environmental Benefits

High Protein

Rich in protein and essential amino acids — a perfect meat alternative for plant-based diets.


Immune Boost

Contains beta-glucans and antioxidants that support immune function and reduce inflammation.


Low Water Use

Uses 90% less water than traditional crops. A sustainable choice for food production.


Zero Waste

Spent substrate becomes nutrient-rich compost. The entire cycle is circular and waste-free.
